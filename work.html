AI Review Analysis System

A concept for restaurants. After each visit, every guest gets the same short feedback form — never filtered by whether their experience was good or bad. AI reads all of it, positive and negative, to find patterns (say, wait-time complaints clustering on Friday nights), and the owner gets a periodic report with actionable recommendations.

Urgent issues
An allergen, a foreign object or a pest triggers an immediate alert, separate from the regular report.
Public reviews
Every guest is invited to leave a Google review in the same way, whatever their feedback said. No review gating — steering only happy guests to public reviews is prohibited by the FTC.

Coffee Shop Scheduling Concept

A staff-scheduling concept for a local coffee shop. The week is a grid of shifts coloured by time of day — opening, mid and closing, from light to dark — and each shift shows who else is working it.

Shift swaps
Employees request a swap with a coworker straight from their own schedule.
Manager inbox
Every request lands in an approval inbox where the manager approves or declines it.
Palette
Black, cream and a burgundy accent — the project’s own identity, not ours.
