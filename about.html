What we believe

Four things that shape how we build.

Systems over slideware

We measure success by what's running in production, not by how good the pitch deck looks.

Right-sized automation

Not everything needs an AI agent. We recommend the simplest system that reliably solves the problem.

Built to be maintained

Every system we hand off comes with documentation your team can actually use.

Miami-based, hands-on

The founder

Who's building it.

I'm the founder of Miami AI Solutions. Before starting the company, I worked as an engineer at a travel technology company, where I designed and deployed a fully autonomous AI agent that generates qualified B2B leads 24/7, along with an automated accounting workflow that eliminated a manual daily process. I've also independently designed and built Carga, a full-stack platform that helps import businesses evaluate sourcing decisions, currently piloting with a real business. I'm completing my Computer Science degree at Florida International University, and started Miami AI Solutions to bring that same hands-on approach to AI and automation to other growing businesses.
